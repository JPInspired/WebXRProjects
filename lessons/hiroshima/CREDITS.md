# Hiroshima, 6 August 1945: credits

Everything in this lesson is made in code for this project: the city, the Industrial Promotion Hall (the Atomic
Bomb Dome), the Aioi Bridge, the Peace Memorial Park, the trams, trees, smoke, the cloud, the sky, every texture
(drawn on canvases in `src/world/textures.js`) and every sound (`src/audio.js`). There are no models, pictures or
sound files from anywhere else.

The layout is simplified from the real city: the rivers, the T-shaped bridge, the hall, Shima Hospital (the
hypocentre) and the park's axis are where they are, at about their real distances; the houses and streets are
representative, not a survey.

Facts follow the accounts of the Hiroshima Peace Memorial Museum and the City of Hiroshima. The Greek text was
written for this project and should be checked by a Greek speaker before use.

The lesson was asked for to accompany *The Flowers of Hiroshima* by Edita Morris (1959). It quotes nothing from the
novel; the discussion questions only refer to its setting and themes.

## Photo textures (26 Nov 2026)

Five colour maps from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), **CC0** (public domain, no attribution needed), 1k JPG,
in `textures/`: `grey_roof_tiles` (roofs), `dark_wooden_planks` (laid over the drawn house fronts),
`japanese_stone_wall` (river embankments), `dirt` (the ground in 1945, 1958 and today) and `burned_ground_01`
(the ground after the bomb). The drawn versions are still used for everything else.
