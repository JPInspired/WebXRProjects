# Credits

Everything in the Pendulum Lab (the protractor rig, the giant pendulum, the stopwatch and light gate, the energy
display, the clock, swing, pirate ship and metronome, the class board and the sounds) is made in code for this
project. The lesson follows the original Inspired Education EngageVR lesson "Pendulum" (a large protractor with
the pendulum in front of it, grab and release the weight, up and down buttons for the length).
