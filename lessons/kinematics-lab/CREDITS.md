# Credits

Everything in the Kinematics Lab (the drop tower and its life-size measuring pole, the pendulum, spring and ramp,
the chart, the datasheet, the class board and the sounds) is made in code for this project. The lesson follows
the original Inspired Education EngageVR lesson "Kinematics" (a measuring pole with a glass balcony at 5, 10 and
15 m, a ball to drop, a chart of 20 samples and a datasheet), extended to the pendulums and springs in its lesson
plan.
