# Credits

Everything in the Voltaic Lab (the beakers, metal strips, bottles, salt bridges, multimeter, the particle view,
the cut-away batteries, the class board and the sounds) is made in code for this project. The lesson follows
the original Inspired Education EngageVR lesson plan "Voltaic Lab".

Chemistry: standard electrode potentials are textbook values at 25 °C. The voltages of simple cells (two metals
in one electrolyte) and their internal resistances are simplified estimates chosen to behave like real school
cells (for example about 0.9 V for zinc and copper in a lemon, with only a tiny current).
