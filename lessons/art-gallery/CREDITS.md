# Credits: Art Gallery

The twelve paintings in `paintings/` are photographs of public-domain works of art (every artist died more than 70 years ago), from Wikimedia Commons, where each file is marked **public domain**. They were shrunk to at most 1600 pixels on their longest side (WebP) for the headset. Picasso's *Guernica* (1937), in the original lesson plan, is still in copyright (until 2044) and is not included: Goya's *The Third of May 1808*, which it is often compared with, is shown instead.
