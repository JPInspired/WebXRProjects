# Credits

Everything in Circular Motion (the kit, the balls, the stopwatch and light gate, the Earth and satellite, the car
on its bend, the class board and the sounds, made with the Web Audio API) is made in code for this project. The
lesson follows the original Inspired Education EngageVR lesson plan "Circular Motion" (balls of 50 to 250 g on
strings, spun and timed with a stopwatch), using the standard school centripetal-force kit (string, tube and
hanging weight) so that the pull stays fixed.

The Earth's continents are simple made-up shapes for a teaching model, not a map.
