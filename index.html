Lessons you can step into.

Hold a beating heart, build a PC part by part, swirl a ball above your head and time it, or walk through the great hall of the Natural History Museum. 20 hands-on lessons in science, computing and museum visits, straight in the headset’s browser, for one student or a whole class at once.

Pick a lesson and start a shared session. Students join with a four-letter code; in the same room, everyone’s lab lines up with the real classroom.

Teach from your own menu

Choose the activity and level for everyone, put predictions on the class board and reveal the answers, while each student works on their own bench.
