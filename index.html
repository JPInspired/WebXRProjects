Immersive Learning for the Modern Classroom

Lessons You Can Step Into

Twenty-one hands-on lessons in science, computing, art and the humanities, designed to bring difficult ideas within reach. Students hold a beating heart, assemble a computer and stand beneath a blue whale, directly in the headset’s browser, whether learning independently or as a whole class.

Open on Any Headset

Visit this page in the Meta Quest browser or on any WebXR headset. There is nothing to install, and every lesson also works on a computer screen.

Bring the Class Together

Choose a lesson and start a class session. Students enter its four-character code at the top of this page and, in the same room, every lab aligns with the real classroom.

Lead From the Teacher Menu

Set the activity and level for everyone, gather predictions on the class board and reveal the answers, while each student works at their own bench.
