Teacher Guides

A guide for every lesson in the library, setting out its learning aims, how each activity runs and practical tips for teaching it with a class.

Before a Shared Lesson

  • Open the lesson once on a headset before class, so you know where everything is.
  • Charge the headsets and switch them on before the lesson.

During the Lesson

  • Students join with the six-character code at the top of the lessons page.
  • The Teacher Menu’s Class tab can pause the class, bring your bench in front of every student (Watch My Bench) and run a timer.
  • Once everyone has joined, Close the Class (Students tab) keeps anyone else out.
  • A headset that drops out rejoins by itself, so there is no need to restart the session.
